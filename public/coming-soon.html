Water and sand · rotating wheel

A one-metre circular drum with short disconnected baffles. Drag the rim to rotate it, switch between water and sand, and choose raw simulation particles or the material embedding. The two materials share the wheel geometry but keep separate constitutive laws and World state.

Starts paused: drag to position the frozen configuration, then press Play. Sand uses the granular particle-law World and a density-weighted lane embedding; Flow width changes the falling stream without changing its deposited area. The water-based Liquid-sand test remains available for comparison. These are evolving physics previews, not finished material models.Open wheel full page

Dry sand · spinning hourglass

Drag to rotate or flip the glass. Coarse material parcels release fractional area when the throat passes represented grains, even when the opening is smaller than a parcel. The same granular discharge Law drives the CPU reference and GPU World; a repose Law shapes the deposited layer, and a separate Embedding draws the compact and flowing sand without exposing simulation cells.

Experimental GPU geometry adapter, not yet a compiler-emitted VKF application. Use Throat to test the one-grain limit, Pile angle to vary repose, and Check volume to audit the material ledger.Open hourglass full page

Five stones · lift and drop

Five independently generated fieldstones now represent gray granite, basalt, red granite, quartzite and banded gneiss. Each has its own proportion, irregular silhouette, density, inertia and seamless object-space mineral texture. Touch a stone, lift it vertically and release it into the pile.

Contacts use irregular support geometry, lever-correct Coulomb friction, low stone restitution, bounded rolling/spin resistance, and contact-qualified GPU sleeping. Momentum and angular response pass through every collision.Open stones full page

Tree, lawn and wind · procedural generation

An eight-metre generated tree above a dense lawn. Swipe inside the view to orbit, choose a seeded branch-and-leaf distribution, and adjust air speed between 1 and 8 m/s. The particle switch reveals parcels exchanging momentum with the scene. Air interaction and elastic response remain separate from shape generation.

Set coherent wind continuously from 0 to 20 m/s. 64,000 particles carry divergence-free gusts and boundary-layer shear; porous foliage drag transfers equal opposite impulse into material-aware branch and leaf modes.Open tree full page

Executable VKF application

Tree, lawn, elastic nodes and inlet parcels are added layers of one World. GPU parcel impacts drive the local spring response; the embedding reads that state directly, without an animated wind wave.
